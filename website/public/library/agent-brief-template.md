# Agent Brief Template

Version 1.1 · Kramaniti Kosh

## Intended outcome

An accountable agent brief with a bounded job and a clear handoff.

## Before you begin

- One recurring job and its outcome
- Approved inputs and exclusions
- An owner and a receiving person or team

## How to use

1. Fill in the brief with the workflow owner before choosing tools or a model.
2. Specify the output format, source boundary, stop conditions and receiving owner.
3. Use the brief as instructions in your chosen environment and test it with a safe sample. Record that test before relying on it.

## Working template

## Role

What recurring job does this agent support? Name the business outcome, not a vague capability.

## Inputs

- Approved source material
- Decision context
- Constraints and exclusions

## Output contract

Describe the exact result, format, audience, and quality standard.

## Boundaries

- What the agent must not decide or change
- What requires human review
- What sources it may use
- When it must escalate

## Handoff

Name the next owner, the decision they need to make, and the evidence they should receive.

## Demonstration

This is an illustrative scenario, not a client case study or a measured result. Do not reuse its details as facts about your work.

An editorial team wants help preparing a review brief from approved draft notes.

### Sample inputs

Demonstration inputs only: approved draft notes are available; the editorial lead makes publication decisions; the publisher receives approved copy.

### Example output

#### Job
Prepare an editorial review brief from approved draft notes.

#### Inputs
Only the attached draft and approved reference notes.

#### Output contract
Return a summary, unresolved claims and questions for the editorial lead.

#### Boundaries
Do not invent evidence, choose a publication date, edit live systems or publish. If references are missing, label the gap and stop the affected claim.

#### Handoff
[Name the editorial lead] reviews the brief. The publisher receives copy only after explicit approval.

#### Sample test
Include one unsupported claim in a safe sample. Expected behaviour: flag the claim and request evidence. Test result: not yet recorded.

## Quality check

The job is specific; inputs and exclusions are explicit; the sample test includes a stop condition; the receiving owner is named.

## Limits and human review

A well-written brief does not prove runtime reliability or grant access to tools. Test it in the intended environment. Keep consequential actions with the named human owner.
