# Write in the founder’s voice without the founder rewriting it

Version 1.1 · Kramaniti Kosh

> How to use this file: fill in the Working template section below. It opens in any text editor, or you can paste the whole file into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and ask it to help you fill it in for your business.

## Intended outcome

One short voice sheet with real examples of writing that sounds like the founder, examples that do not, and a few plain rules, so that staff and AI tools can draft emails, proposals, posts and replies that the founder only needs to check, not rewrite line by line.

## Before you begin

- Five to ten pieces of writing the founder is happy with: emails, proposals, posts, WhatsApp replies or website text
- Three to five drafts the founder rewrote or rejected, with the founder's version next to them if you have it
- Fifteen minutes with the founder to say what they liked, what they changed and why
- The kinds of writing staff or AI draft most often in a normal week
- One person who will keep the sheet up to date and collect new examples

## How to use

1. Sit with the founder and go through the examples. For each one, write a line on what makes it sound right or wrong. Use the founder's own words where you can. Do not write rules nobody has seen the founder apply.
2. Fill in the sheet, keeping each rule short and pairing it with an example. Then give the sheet to the people and AI tools that draft for the business. If you use ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, paste the sheet in before asking for a draft, and ask the tool to say which rule it followed for any line it is unsure about.
3. For the next two weeks, the founder marks up drafts against the sheet instead of rewriting them. Every time the founder changes a line, add the before and after to the sheet. Check the whole sheet again on the review date.

## Things a draft must never do

- Promise a date, price, discount or result the founder has not agreed
- Name a client, project or person without the founder's go-ahead
- Make claims about the business that nobody can point to a source for
- Copy another business's words or style closely

## Stop rules

- No rule goes on the sheet unless it comes from a real example the founder agreed with.
- If a draft needs a fact, price or promise the sheet does not give, the drafter asks a person instead of guessing.
- If the founder rewrites the same kind of line twice, add a rule and an example before the next draft.
- If the next review date has passed, the founder reads the whole sheet before anyone relies on it again.
- The sheet guides how things are said. It does not decide what the business promises or claims.

## Demonstration

This is an illustrative scenario, not a client case study or a measured result. Do not reuse its details as facts about your work.

A sample solo-founder interior design studio in Bengaluru designs homes and small offices. The founder meets every client and signs off every design. A junior designer and an AI tool now draft client emails, project updates and Instagram captions, and the founder has been rewriting most of them late at night.

### Sample inputs

Demonstration inputs only. No real clients, projects, prices or results are used. From the founder's sent folder: a project update that starts with what is done this week and ends with one question for the client; a welcome email that is warm but short; a caption that describes one material choice and why. Rewritten drafts: an AI caption calling a flat "a luxurious sanctuary of timeless elegance", which the founder changed to a plain line about the wood and the light; an update that apologised three times for a delay, which the founder cut to one clear line with the new date the contractor had confirmed. From the conversation: the founder writes to clients by first name, keeps updates to a few short paragraphs, and never posts a client's home without written permission.

### Example output

#### How the founder sounds, in plain words
[Fact within this demonstration] Says what is done first, then what is next, then asks one question. Describes materials and light plainly instead of using praise words. Apologises once, gives the reason and the new date, and moves on.

#### Words and phrases the founder never uses
[Fact within this demonstration] "Luxurious", "timeless elegance", "sanctuary", "dream home", and any line that claims the studio is the best.

#### When the founder still checks before it goes out
[Fact within this demonstration] Any post that shows a client's home, any update about a delay or extra cost, and the first email to every new client.

#### Next draft checked against the sheet
[Inference] The junior designer's draft caption for a finished study room describes the cane shutters and the morning light, and names no client. It follows the sheet, so the founder only needs to confirm that the client's written permission to post is on file.

#### Stop rules applied
[Recommendation] For the next two weeks the founder marks up drafts against the sheet rather than rewriting them, and the junior designer adds every change to the change log. This recommendation is not the business's decision.

## Quality check

Every rule comes from a real example the founder agreed with; the sheet has at least three on-voice and two off-voice examples; the never-do list covers promises, client names and unsupported claims; the founder-checks list covers new clients, money, complaints and public posts; the sheet names a keeper, a last checked date and a next review date; every change is in the change log.

## Limits and human review

The sheet helps people and AI sound like the founder; it does not make a draft accurate, and it does not decide what the business promises or claims. The person sending each draft checks its facts and its tone against the sheet, and the founder still checks the kinds of writing listed above. To decide which other work needs the founder's review and which does not, use [Decide when the founder needs to review work](/kosh/conditional-founder-review-rules/). A filled template is not approval to send, post or make claims in the founder's name. Keep consequential actions with the named human owner.
