# Decide when a customer chat gets handed to a person

Version 1.1 · Kramaniti Kosh

> How to use this file: fill in the Working template section below. It opens in any text editor, or you can paste the whole file into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and ask it to help you fill it in for your business.

## Intended outcome

A one-page plan that lists the situations where a chat assistant, website chat or WhatsApp auto-reply must stop and pass the customer to a person, who picks it up and how fast, what the customer is told while they wait, and what context goes with the handoff, agreed before the assistant talks to any customer.

## Before you begin

- The channels where customers reach you today (WhatsApp, website chat, email, phone) and who answers each one
- A few weeks of real customer messages, including the awkward ones: complaints, refund asks, angry replies
- One person who can decide who picks up each kind of handoff, and when they are actually available

## How to use

1. Go through the real messages with whoever answers customers today. Mark every message where a person had to step in, and write down why.
2. Fill in the plan: the handoff situations, who picks each one up, how quickly, what the customer is told, and what the person receiving it needs to see.
3. Test it on past messages before switching on any assistant. Check the plan again after the first two weeks of live use, and update it whenever a handoff goes wrong.

## Stop rules

- Do not switch on an assistant for customers until every row in the situations table has a person and a backup.
- Do not promise a reply time the team cannot keep. If nobody is available, the assistant says so plainly.
- Refunds, discounts, safety questions, damage claims and anything that sounds like a legal or medical question go to a person every time.
- If a handoff was missed or went wrong, record it and fix the plan before the next week of use. Do not invent counts or reply times you have not measured.

## Demonstration

This is an illustrative scenario, not a client case study or a measured result. Do not reuse its details as facts about your work.

A sample six-person home cleaning and pest control service in Bengaluru wants a WhatsApp assistant to answer booking questions. The owner takes most calls, two supervisors run the field teams, and one office person handles bookings. Nobody has written down when the assistant should stop and pass a customer to a person.

### Sample inputs

Demonstration inputs only. No message counts, reply times, booking numbers or prices are used. From a talk with the owner and the office person: customers ask about slots, prices and what chemicals are used; some messages come in late at night; a few customers have complained about missed spots or a stain after a deep clean; the owner is the only one who agrees refunds; the office person works daytime hours on weekdays and Saturday.

#### What the customer is told
During hours: "I’m passing this to [Name], who will reply to you here shortly."
Outside hours: "Our team is offline right now. [Name] will reply to you here in the morning."
Urgent: "Please call [number] now so someone can help you directly."

#### What goes with the handoff
The customer’s name and number, the address or area, the job date, what the assistant already said, and which situation triggered the handoff.

#### Stop rules applied
[Recommendation] Keep refunds, damage claims and chemical safety answers with named people, and test the plan on past WhatsApp messages before the assistant replies to anyone live. This recommendation is not the business’s decision.

## Quality check

Every handoff situation has a named person and a backup; the reply time is one the team says it can keep; the customer is told honestly what happens next; the handoff carries enough context that the customer does not repeat themselves; refunds, safety and damage stay with people; no counts or reply times were invented.

## Limits and human review

The plan sets out when and how a customer is passed to a person; it does not prove that an assistant is accurate, safe or right for your business, and it does not check consumer, data protection or safety rules. A filled plan is not approval to switch on an assistant, agree refunds or answer safety questions automatically. Keep consequential actions with the named human owner.
